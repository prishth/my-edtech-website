CMAT 2027: Exam Date, Registration, Eligibility, Pattern, Syllabus, Fees, Application Process & More The Common Management Admission Test (CMAT) is a national level entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) for admission to management programmes in India. The examination is used by participating management institutes to shortlist candidates for programmes such as MBA, PGDM and other management courses. If you are planning to appear for CMAT 2027, understanding the examination well in advance can make the preparation process much easier.CMAT 2027 will be conducted by NTA as three hour entrance examination in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode to evaluate the candidates’ ability across various segments like Quantitative Technique, Logical Reasoning, Language Comprehension and General Awareness, for admission to the Management Courses in the Academic Session 2027 -28.

What is CMAT? The Common Management Admission Test (CMAT) is a national level entrance examination for admission to management programmes in India.

The examination was conducted by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) until 2018. From 2019 onwards, NTA has been responsible for conducting CMAT. According to the official bulletin, CMAT scores are accepted by AICTE-approved institutions, university departments, constituent colleges and affiliated colleges that participate in the examination.

However, there is an important distinction that CMAT aspirants should understand:

NTA conducts CMAT, but it does not conduct the admission process for individual colleges.

Candidates have to apply separately to the institutions in which they wish to seek admission. Each participating institution may have its own CMAT cutoff and selection process. Depending on the institute, the admission process may include components such as a Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI).CMAT 2027 Eligibility Criteria Based on the official CMAT notification, the eligibility requirements were straightforward.

A candidate:

Must hold a Bachelor's degree in any discipline.

Candidates appearing in the final year of their Bachelor's degree could also apply, provided their result was declared before commencement of admission for the relevant academic session.

Must be a citizen of India.

There was no age restriction for appearing in CMAT 2027.

CMAT Marking Scheme

The marking scheme specified in the CMAT 2026 bulletin was:

+4 marks for every correct answer

  • 1 mark for every incorrect answer

0 marks for an unattempted question

Thus, the maximum raw score was 400 marks.

Is there negative marking in CMAT?

Yes. Under the CMAT 2027 marking scheme, one mark is deducted for every incorrect answer.CMAT 2027 Syllabus

CMAT 2027 Application Form

Based on the CMAT 2027 process, the application was completely online.

Candidates are required to complete the registration and application process through the official NTA CMAT website. Applications submitted through any other mode are not accepted.

The application process broadly consists of four steps.

  • Step 1: Registration

Candidates first have to register for the examination and create their login credentials.

A system-generated Application Number is provided after successful registration. Candidates were advised to preserve this number because it was required for future login and correspondence.

  • Step 2: Fill the Application Form

Candidates then have to enter information such as:

Personal details

Educational qualifications

University/programme information

Test-related details

Examination city preferences

Candidates also have to upload the required images and documents.

  • Step 3: Pay the Examination Fee

The examination fee was payable online through:

Credit Card

Debit Card

Net Banking

UPI

Applicable processing charges and GSTare additional.

  • Step 4: Download the Confirmation Page

The application is considered successfully submitted only after successful payment of the prescribed fee.

Candidates are advised to download, save and print the Confirmation Page for future reference.Documents Required for CMAT Application

The CMAT 2027 application requires candidates to upload their photograph and signature. PwD/PwBD candidates also had to upload the applicable certificate.

Photograph

The official instructions specified that the photograph should:

Be recent

Be in JPG/JPEG format

Have approximately 80% of the face visible

CMAT 2027 Preparation: How Should You Prepare?

CMAT is not simply an examination of academic knowledge. With five sections and negative marking, candidates need a combination of speed, accuracy, awareness and decision-making.

A practical preparation strategy is to divide your preparation into three stages.

Stage 1: Build the Basics

Start by strengthening:

Quantitative concepts

Logical reasoning fundamentals

English grammar and vocabulary

Reading comprehension

Static GK

Current affairs

Entrepreneurship concepts

Do not rush into full length mocks before understanding the fundamentals.

Stage 2: Build Speed

Once the basics are comfortable, start solving questions under time pressure.

Focus on:

Calculation speed

Question selection

Eliminating incorrect options

Avoiding unnecessary calculations

Improving accuracy

Identifying easy questions quickly

Stage 3: Mock Tests and Analysis

Mocks become particularly important closer to the examination. But simply taking mock tests is not enough.

After every mock, analyse:

What did I get wrong?

Why did I get it wrong?

Was it a conceptual mistake, calculation error or reading error?

Did I spend too much time on a difficult question?

Which questions should I have skipped?

This analysis often contributes more to improvement than simply taking another mock.

CMAT 2027 FAQs

  • When will CMAT 2027 be conducted?

The official CMAT 2027 examination date has not been announced in the source available here. Candidates should prepare keeping January 2027 as the tentative date in mind..

  • Who conducts CMAT?

CMAT is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). NTA has been conducting CMAT since 2019.

  • Is there an age limit for CMAT?

Under the CMAT 2026 eligibility criteria, there was no age restriction. The final CMAT 2027 eligibility criteria should be checked once the notification is released.

  • Can final-year graduation students apply for CMAT?

Yes. Under the CMAT 2026 rules, candidates in the final year of their Bachelor's degree could apply, provided their result was declared before commencement of admission for the relevant academic session.

  • Is CMAT conducted online?

Yes. The CMAT 2026 examination was conducted in Computer Based Test (CBT) mode.

  • Is there negative marking in CMAT?

Yes. Under the 2026 scheme, candidates received 4 marks for a correct answer and lost 1 mark for an incorrect answer.

  • Does appearing for CMAT guarantee admission?

No. Candidates must apply separately to participating institutions, meet their respective cutoffs and satisfy their selection criteria. Some institutes may conduct GD and PI as part of the selection process.
