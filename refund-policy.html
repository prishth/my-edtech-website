Refund and Cancellation Policy

When you can cancel, when you get your money back, and exactly how long each step takes.

Effective date: 8 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains when you can cancel, when you get a refund, and how long it takes. Everything we sell is a digital product delivered instantly, so please read the timelines below before you buy.

The short version

Cancel within 24 hours of purchase, provided you have not started the test or downloaded the material, and you get a full refund. Once you begin a paid test or download a paid PDF, the product has been consumed and is not refundable. Payment failures and duplicate charges are always refunded in full.

1. Nature of our products

Prishth sells digital services: online mock tests, mock test bundles, and downloadable study material. There is no physical goods shipment. Access is granted to your account immediately after payment is confirmed. There is no shipping charge and no delivery timeline, because nothing is shipped.

2. Cancellation

  • You may cancel a purchase within 24 hours of payment, provided you have not started any test included in it and have not downloaded any material included in it.
  • To cancel, email ravikant.choubey@prishth.in from your registered email address with your Razorpay Payment ID.
  • Once a test is started, or a PDF is downloaded, that purchase can no longer be cancelled.

3. When you get a full refund

We refund the full amount in these situations:

  • Money debited, access not granted. Payment left your account but your purchase did not activate.
  • Duplicate payment. You were charged twice for the same item.
  • Wrong amount charged. You were charged more than the listed price.
  • Product not delivered. You bought a bundle and the tests were never made available to you.
  • Cancelled within 24 hours and unused, as described above.
  • We withdrew the product before you used it.
  • Prolonged technical failure on our side that prevented you from using what you bought, and we could not fix it. Where you prefer, we will extend your access instead.

4. When we do not refund

We do not refund in these situations:

  • You have already attempted the test or downloaded the material. A digital product that has been consumed cannot be returned.
  • You changed your mind after 24 hours.
  • You did not score as well as you hoped, or you did not clear the examination. Our products are preparation tools and carry no guarantee of a result.
  • You bought the wrong product but have already used it.
  • Your access period expired without you using it.
  • Your account was closed by us for breaching the Terms and Conditions, for example by sharing paid content.
  • You disagree with the answer to a question. Report it to us instead. We review every report and correct genuine errors, but a disputed question is not a ground for refunding an entire purchase.

6. Timelines

StageTimeline

The last step is handled by your bank and by Razorpay, not by us. Delays at that stage are outside our control, though we will help you chase them.

7. How the money comes back

Refunds are always returned to the same payment method you paid with. If you paid by UPI, it returns to that UPI account. If you paid by card, it returns to that card. We cannot refund to a different account, and we do not refund in cash or as site credit unless you specifically ask for an extension of access instead.

8. Partial refunds

Where you bought a bundle and used part of it, and we agree a refund is due for reasons within section 3, we refund on a pro rata basis for the unused portion.

9. Failed payments

If a payment fails but money leaves your account, this is usually a bank hold that reverses on its own within 5 to 7 working days. If it has not reversed after 7 working days, contact us with the Payment ID and we will trace it with Razorpay.

10. Disputes

If you are unhappy with our decision, write to our Grievance Officer at ravikant.choubey@prishth.in. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 days.
