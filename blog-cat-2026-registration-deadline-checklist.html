CAT 2026 Registration Deadline: What You Need to Know

The registration window opened on August 3 and shuts on September 15 at 5:00 PM. IIM Indore, this year's conducting institute, released the official notification on July 26. The exam itself is scheduled for November 29 which is a gap of roughly eleven weeks, which is enough time to actually prepare once the form is out of the way.

The part people misjudge

September 15 doesn't mean you have the whole day. It means the portal stops accepting submissions at 5:00 PM sharp. Traffic spikes in the final 48 hours, payment gateways slow down under load, and "I'll submit in the evening" has cost people their attempt in past cycles. Complete it early, not on the day.

Before You Open the CAT 2026 Application Form, Keep These Ready

Don't open the form and start hunting for documents halfway through as this is how mistakes creep in. Keep these next to you:

01 Academic details

Pull your exact percentages and CGPA straight off the marksheet as these fields lock the moment you submit, so a remembered figure that's off by a decimal becomes a permanent error.

02 Personal information

Match your full name, date of birth, and contact details exactly to your official ID; CAT locks these after submission, so a nickname or shortened version here causes problems later.

03 Photograph

A recent passport-style photo meeting the CAT specifications for size and background, check the current information bulletin for exact dimensions before uploading.

04 Signature

A scanned signature in the specified format, this trips people up more often than the photo does.

05 Category certificate

If you're applying under SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS/PwD; the certificate needs to be valid and correctly categorized.

06 Graduation status

Whether you've graduated, are in your final year, or are awaiting results, the form treats these differently.

07 Work experience details

If applicable the months and organization, entered accurately since this feeds into your profile later.

08 A working payment method

Have a card, net banking login, or UPI ready to go, test it beforehand. Payment gateways under last-day traffic are a bad time to discover your net banking password expired.

CAT 2026 Test City Selection: Don't Treat It as a Throwaway Choice

You'll select five preferred test cities from a list of roughly 170 across the country, ranked in order. IIM Indore tries to allot your top preference, but the final call depends on seat availability at each centre, so there's no way to guarantee a specific one.

The mistake here isn't picking the wrong city as it's not thinking about it at all. Rank by where you can genuinely and comfortably reach on exam day, factoring in travel time and where you're likely to actually be in late November, not just what looks familiar on the screen. There's no known advantage to gaming the order or rank by genuine preference.

What Happens After You Submit the CAT 2026 Form?

Registration→ Application submitted→ Admit card (First week of November)→ CAT 2026 exam (November 29)→ Answer key and result→ IIM selection process

Submitting the form isn't the finish line; it's the starting gun for actual preparation. Once it's done, the next few weeks matter more than the paperwork did.

What If You Miss the CAT 2026 Registration Deadline?

This is the question many students will start searching for on September 15. The safest answer is simple:

Do not plan on an extension

The officially announced CAT 2026 registration deadline is September 15, 2026, at 5:00 PM IST. Current reporting states that no extension has been announced.

CAT has extended registration deadlines in some previous years, but that is not a reason to assume the same will happen in 2026.

If IIM Indore announces an extension, the official CAT website will be the place to verify it. Until then, treat September 15 at 5 PM as the real deadline.

There is another important distinction:

Creating a registration account is not enough

You need to complete the required application process and fee payment within the prescribed window.
