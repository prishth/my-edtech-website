CAT 2025 Question Paper PDF & Analysis

CAT 2025 was conducted by IIM Kozhikode on November 30, 2025, across three slots. This is the most recent and most relevant PYQ set for CAT 2026 aspirants, since it reflects the latest question framing style, RC passage selection, and DILR set complexity.

VARC: Passages leaned into argumentative and inference-heavy non-fiction, alongside the now-standard Para Jumble, Odd Sentence, and Para Summary set.

DILR: A continued shift toward multi-layered data sets that combine logical constraints with data interpretation, rather than pure table-reading.

QA: Balanced across Arithmetic, Algebra, and Geometry/Mensuration, with Arithmetic carrying the heaviest weight; consistent with recent years.

CAT 2024 Question Paper PDF & Analysis

CAT 2024 (IIM Calcutta) is essential practice for understanding the two-hour, 68-question format under real exam conditions.

VARC: A strong mix of philosophy, science, and social-commentary passages, with tightly worded inference-based questions rather than direct-recall ones.

DILR: Sets required careful reading of conditions before attempting, a hallmark of well-designed DILR that CAT 2024 leaned into heavily.

QA: Number systems and Time-Speed-Distance/Work questions appeared more frequently across slots.

CAT 2023 Question Paper PDF & Analysis

CAT 2023 (IIM Lucknow) is widely regarded by aspirants as one of the better-calibrated recent papers for benchmarking your VARC and DILR speed. CAT 2023 had 66 questions (24 VARC, 22 QA & 20 LRDI )

VARC: Slightly longer passages than 2024/2025, with a good spread of L1/L2/L3 difficulty across the section.

DILR: Introduced more real-world, business-context data sets (logistics, scheduling, and resource allocation themes).

QA: Moderate difficulty overall, making it a good "confidence-building" paper to attempt after harder years.

CAT 2022 Question Paper PDF & Analysis

CAT 2022 (IIM Bangalore) marked the transition to the now-standard 68-question, 120-minute format (down from CAT 2021's extended-time, pandemic-era pattern), making it the first paper in the "current era" of CAT.

VARC: Reduced question count per slot compared to earlier years, which raised the accuracy bar per question.

DILR: A noticeable jump in set difficulty compared to 2021, considered one of the tougher DILR years.

QA: Arithmetic-heavy again, with a few notably tricky Geometry questions across slots.

How to Practice CAT PYQs the Right Way (Prishth's Method)

Downloading the papers is the easy part. Here's the framework Prishth's content team recommends, based on how these questions are actually constructed:

Attempt slot-wise, under real timing. 40 minutes per section, no exceptions, this is where most of the "PYQs feel easier than mocks" illusion gets corrected.

Review every wrong option, not just the right answer. In genuine CAT-level questions, every incorrect option fails for one specific, nameable reason. Understanding why an option is wrong builds far more skill than just confirming the right one.

Work backward from 2025 to 2021. Start with the most recent paper to calibrate to the current pattern, then move to older years to build volume and topic coverage.

Track your VARC passage-type accuracy separately. Note your hit rate by passage theme (philosophy, science, business, social policy), this reveals blind spots generic score reports miss.

Re-attempt after 3-4 weeks. A second pass on the same PYQs, after your fundamentals improve, shows real percentile-level progress far better than a fresh mock.

CAT PYQ Difficulty Level: How Prishth Rates the Papers

Not all CAT questions demand the same level of thinking, calculation, or time. To make the difficulty analysis more useful, Prishth categorizes questions and papers into three levels of difficulty: Easy, Moderate, and Difficult. Our LOD assessment considers not just the concept involved, but also the reasoning required, calculation load, complexity of the approach, and the time pressure a candidate may face while solving the question.

Prishth LOD Framework

Easy Questions that can generally be solved using familiar concepts with relatively low calculation or reasoning demands.

Moderate Questions requiring sound conceptual understanding, multiple steps or careful option elimination.

Difficult Questions involving substantial reasoning, lengthy calculations, less obvious approaches or significant time pressure.

Overall Slot Difficulty

The overall LOD should not simply be the average of three section labels. It should reflect the combined difficulty and time pressure of the paper, including question selection requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are CAT PYQs enough to prepare for CAT 2026?

CAT PYQs should be the backbone of your practice, but they work best combined with topic-wise practice and full-length mocks that simulate new, unseen questions.

How many years of CAT PYQs should I solve?

At minimum, CAT 2021-2025 (the current-format era).

Should I solve CAT PYQs slot-wise or as a full paper?

Both. Solve slot-wise first to calibrate section-by-section timing, then attempt a full slot end-to-end closer to your exam date to simulate real exam fatigue.

Where can I get CAT PYQs with accurate solutions?

In this page we have provided question papers along with answer keys.
