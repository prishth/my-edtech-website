About Prishth Technosoft

An Indian education technology company publishing free previous year papers, running an online test series, and writing original exam content for coaching institutes.

Effective date: 8 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 8 September 2026

Prishth Technosoft Pvt Ltd is an Indian education technology company. We build free previous year question paper archives and online test series for candidates preparing for competitive examinations, and we produce original examination content for coaching institutes.

What we do

Prishth operates on two sides of the same business.

For students. We publish previous year question papers as free downloads with no login and no payment, across management, law, banking, SSC and railway examinations. We also run an online test series with sectional timing, automatic scoring, full solutions and section wise analysis.

For coaching institutes. We write original examination content on contract: question banks, mock tests, solutions and study material. We currently serve more than 30 business clients. Our content is written fresh and checked for originality before delivery.

Who we are

Prishth is run by people who write competitive examination material for a living. The papers, solutions and explanations on this site are produced in house by subject matter experts, not scraped or reworded from other sources.

What we believe

  • Previous year papers should be free. They are the single most useful preparation resource a candidate has, and putting them behind a signup wall helps nobody.
  • Content should be original. Every paper and solution we publish or deliver is written from scratch.
  • Accuracy over volume. A wrong answer key costs a candidate more than a missing one.
