The Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) is a national-level entrance exam for admission to undergraduate and postgraduate law programmes offered by National Law Universities across India, organised by the Consortium of National Law Universities.

All admissions to the five-year integrated LLB and LLM programmes commencing in the academic year 2027–2028 will be through CLAT 2027. Solving previous year papers is one of the most reliable ways to understand the exam's nature, difficulty and requirements.

🎯Why Solving Previous Year CLAT Papers Is Crucial

Studying CLAT previous year papers offers real insight into the exam's structure, difficulty and question types. Here is why they belong in every aspirant's strategy:

  • Identify repeated patternsAnalysing past papers reveals frequently tested topics and evolving question styles.
  • Improve accuracyRegular practice sharpens your ability to answer correctly under pressure, reducing negative marking.
  • Simulate the exam environmentSolving recent papers keeps you aligned with the current structure and marking scheme.
  • Track progressWorking through papers from earlier years lets you measure improvement over time.

✅How to Use CLAT Previous Year Papers Effectively

Having access to the papers is one thing; using them well is another. Here is how to get the most from them:

  • Start early — begin solving papers 6 to 8 months before the exam to get familiar with the format.
  • Simulate exam conditions — practise in a quiet room with a timer to build time-management skills.
  • Analyse solutions — after each paper, review your answers, understand mistakes and revise related topics.
  • Focus on trends — identify recurring topics; CLAT often emphasises reading comprehension and current legal developments.
  • Combine with mock tests — pair past papers with full-length mocks for well-rounded preparation.

❓Frequently Asked Questions — CLAT Previous Year Papers

How many years of CLAT papers are available here?

Eight years of CLAT previous year question papers, from 2019 to 2026, are available to download free.

Are these CLAT question papers free to download?

Yes. Every paper on this page is free and requires no sign-up, login or email.

How many CLAT papers should I solve?

Aim to solve at least the last five years' papers, since they reflect the current pattern most closely. Older papers add useful extra practice on question quality.

Do older CLAT papers still match the current pattern?

The core structure has stayed consistent, though the exam is now fully passage-based across most sections. Older papers remain valuable for practice; just keep the current 120-question, 2-hour format in mind.

Can solving past papers alone guarantee a good CLAT score?

No. Past papers are essential, but they work best alongside conceptual clarity, regular mock tests and a thorough understanding of the syllabus.
