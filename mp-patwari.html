MP Patwari Recruitment 2026: Notification, Exam Date, Vacancy, Eligibility, Syllabus, Exam Pattern, Salary & Selection Process

MP Patwari Recruitment 2026 is being conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) under the Group-2 Subgroup-4 Joint Recruitment Examination 2026. The official rulebook contains separate provisions for recruitment to the Patwari post under the Department of Land Resources Management, Madhya Pradesh.

According to the official notification, the MP Patwari 2026 application process began on August 4, 2026, while the last date to submit the online application is August 18, 2026. Candidates can make corrections in their applications up to August 23, 2026. MP Patwari Eligibility Criteria 2026

Candidates must satisfy the eligibility conditions prescribed specifically for the Patwari post.

  • Citizenship

The candidate must be:

A citizen of India; and

A local resident of Madhya Pradesh.

The Patwari-specific eligibility provision clearly requires Madhya Pradesh local residency.

  • Educational Qualification

Candidates must possess a graduation degree from a recognised university.

Therefore, candidates who have completed graduation from a recognised university can meet the educational qualification requirement, subject to satisfying the other conditions of the notification.

  • Age Limit (as on 01 January 2026)

Unreserved/General (MP domicile) : Between 18 years & 40 years

SC / ST / OBC / Women / Govt. employees (Corporation/Board/Autonomous body/Home Guard): Between 18 years & 45 years

  • CPCT Qualification

This is one of the most important requirements for MP Patwari recruitment.

Candidates are required to have a valid CPCT scorecard, including:

Hindi typing; and

Computer proficiency.

The notification states that CPCT qualification with Hindi typing and computer proficiency is mandatory for the Patwari selection examination.

What if a selected candidate has not passed CPCT?

The rulebook provides an important provision: if a selected candidate has not passed CPCT, the candidate must pass the CPCT examination within three years after appointment during the probationary period. Otherwise, the appointment can be cancelled.

Candidates should therefore treat CPCT as an important eligibility for MP Patwari Exam requirement rather than something that can simply be ignored.

Pay Scale / Salary of Patwari Post

Pay Band: ₹5,200 – ₹20,200 + Grade Pay ₹2,100

For direct recruits, salary during the first three years of service will be paid as per the Madhya Pradesh General Administration Department order dated 12 December 2019 (applicable fixed remuneration during the probation/initial period, before the candidate moves to the regular pay band + grade pay structure).

How to Apply for MP Patwari Recruitment 2026 (Step-by-Step)

Follow these 9 steps to successfully apply for MP Patwari 2026:

Visit the official MPESB website: esb.mp.gov.in.

Locate the notification for "Group-2 Sub-Group-4 Joint Recruitment Exam 2026 – Patwari (Divyang Category)."

Register/log in using your MP Online credentials or as a registered citizen user.

Fill in personal, educational, and category details carefully.

Upload scanned copies of the required documents/photograph/signature as specified.

Pay the applicable exam fee plus MP Online portal fee.

Review and submit the application form before 18 August 2026.

Use the correction window (till 23 August 2026) to fix any errors in the submitted form.

Download and save the confirmation/application number for future reference — it is required to download the admit card later.

MP Patwari Preparation Strategy

Because the paper contains 200 marks across eight broad subjects, candidates should avoid preparing only one or two sections.

A balanced strategy is better.

  • Build your basics first

Start with:

Mathematics fundamentals

Hindi grammar

MP Patwari 2026 FAQs

  • When will the MP Patwari 2026 exam be conducted?

The MP Patwari examination under the Group-2 Subgroup-4 Joint Recruitment Examination 2026 is scheduled to begin from September 22, 2026.

  • How many Patwari vacancies are there in MP Patwari 2026?

The official Patwari-specific recruitment provision advertises 200 posts.

  • What is the educational qualification for MP Patwari?

Candidates must have a graduation degree from a recognised university.

  • Is CPCT mandatory for MP Patwari?

Yes. The Patwari-specific eligibility conditions require a CPCT scorecard with Hindi typing and computer proficiency.

  • What is the age limit for MP Patwari 2026?

For Madhya Pradesh permanent residents, the prescribed age range is 18 to 40 years (5 Years Relaxation has been given to reserved category), with age calculated as on January 1, 2026.

  • What is the MP Patwari exam pattern?

The examination has 200 marks. The first 100 marks cover General Science, Hindi, English and Mathematics, while the second 100 marks cover General Knowledge & Aptitude, Computer Knowledge, Reasoning and General Management.

  • What is the MP Patwari exam duration?

The examination duration is 3 hours.

  • What is the minimum qualifying percentage?

The Patwari-specific provision states a minimum qualifying requirement of 40%.

  • Can I change my district preference after submitting the application?

No. The notification states that district preference cannot be changed after submission of the application.

  • Will I get posting in my home tehsil?

No. The notification specifically states that selected candidates will not be posted in their home tehsil.

  • What is the MP Patwari salary?

The notification specifies a Patwari pay scale of ₹5,200–20,200 + ₹2,100 Grade Pay, with the remuneration during the first three years governed by the applicable state government order.

  • What subjects are included in the MP Patwari syllabus?

The official syllabus includes General Science, General Hindi, General English, General Mathematics, General Knowledge & Aptitude, General Computer Knowledge, General Logical Reasoning and General Management.
