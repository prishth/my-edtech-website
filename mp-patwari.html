MP Patwari — Madhya Pradesh Patwari Exam

Conducted by the MP Employees Selection Board (MPESB) to recruit Patwaris for the state revenue department — one of Madhya Pradesh’s most popular government jobs.

200Questions
180 minDuration
NoNegative Marking
MPESBConducting Body

The MP Patwari exam is a state-level recruitment test conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB, formerly MP Vyapam) to appoint Patwaris in the state revenue department. A Patwari maintains land records, assists in revenue collection and supports land administration at the village level — a stable, respected government role that draws lakhs of applicants each cycle.

✅MP Patwari Eligibility Criteria

  • NationalityCandidate must be a citizen of India; domicile of Madhya Pradesh is required for reserved-category benefits.
  • Educational QualificationBachelor’s degree from a recognised university, along with CPCT (Computer Proficiency) certification as required by MPESB.
  • Age LimitGenerally 18 to 40 years, with age relaxation for reserved categories as per MP government rules.
  • Computer ProficiencyA valid CPCT score card is typically required at the time of application or appointment.

Note: Always confirm the exact eligibility, age limits and CPCT requirement from the official MPESB notification for the relevant recruitment cycle.

📊MP Patwari Exam Pattern

The MP Patwari exam is a single computer-based paper of 200 questions carrying 200 marks, to be completed in 180 minutes. There is no negative marking. The paper covers the following areas:

SectionFocus
General Knowledge & Current AffairsNational, international and Madhya Pradesh-specific GK and current events.
General Aptitude / ReasoningLogical reasoning, series, analogies, coding-decoding, problem solving.
General MathematicsArithmetic, percentages, ratio, averages, data interpretation up to Class 10 level.
General ScienceBasic physics, chemistry, biology and everyday science.
General HindiGrammar, comprehension and language usage.
General EnglishGrammar, vocabulary and comprehension.
General Computer KnowledgeFundamentals of computers, MS Office and basic IT awareness.

💰MP Patwari Salary & Job Profile

The MP Patwari post falls under pay level 5 of the Madhya Pradesh pay matrix, with a basic pay in the region of ₹24,000 per month plus applicable allowances (dearness allowance, house rent allowance and others), taking the gross salary higher. Beyond pay, the role offers job security, a respected position in the revenue administration, and clear avenues for departmental promotion.

A Patwari’s core duties include maintaining and updating land records, measuring and mapping land, assisting in revenue collection, and supporting various government schemes at the village and tehsil level.

❓Frequently Asked Questions - MP Patwari

Who conducts the MP Patwari exam?

The MP Patwari exam is conducted by the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), formerly known as MP Vyapam.

What is the MP Patwari exam pattern?

It is a single online computer-based paper of 200 questions carrying 200 marks, to be completed in 180 minutes, with no negative marking. It covers GK, reasoning, maths, science, Hindi, English and computer knowledge.

Is there negative marking in the MP Patwari exam?

No. The MP Patwari exam has no negative marking, so candidates should attempt every question.

What is the educational qualification for MP Patwari?

A bachelor's degree from a recognised university is required, along with CPCT (Computer Proficiency Certification Test) as specified by MPESB.

What is the salary of an MP Patwari?

The post falls under pay level 5 of the MP pay matrix, with basic pay around Rs 24,000 per month plus allowances, taking the gross salary higher.
