CLAT 2027 - Exam Overview, Eligibility, Fees, Syllabus, Exam Dates & More. The Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) is India's most popular national-level entrance exam for admission to undergraduate and postgraduate law programmes offered by 26 National Law Universities (NLUs) and several other participating universities. It is conducted every year by the Consortium of National Law Universities (Consortium of NLUs).

For undergraduate admissions, CLAT serves as the gateway to 5-year integrated law programmes such as BA LLB, BBA LLB, BCom LLB, BSc LLB, and BSW LLB. In addition to NLUs, many leading private law colleges and universities also accept CLAT scores for admission.

The CLAT exam tests a candidate's reading comprehension, logical reasoning, legal aptitude, current affairs, and quantitative ability through a passage-based question format. Rather than focusing on rote learning, the exam evaluates analytical thinking, problem-solving, and decision-making skills that are essential for studying law.

CLAT 2027 Exam Highlights

Every year, over one lakh candidates appear for the CLAT exam, making it one of the most competitive law entrance exams in India. A good score in CLAT 2027 can open the door to some of the country's top law schools and excellent career opportunities in litigation, corporate law, judiciary, civil services, legal consulting, and public policy. CLAT 2027 Eligibility Criteria (UG)

CLAT 2027 Eligibility Criteria (UG) If you're planning to appear for CLAT 2027, here are the eligibility requirements you need to meet:

  1. There is no upper age limit to apply for the CLAT 2027 UG exam.
  2. You must have passed Class 12 (10+2) or an equivalent examination with:
  3. At least 45% marks (General, OBC, and other categories).
  4. At least 40% marks for SC, ST, and PwD candidates.

Note: Students who are appearing for their Class 12 board exams in March/April 2027 are also eligible to apply for CLAT 2027. Such candidates must submit proof of passing the qualifying examination during the admission process. If they fail to do so, they will not be considered for admission.

CLAT 2027 Syllabus (Complete Section-wise Syllabus) The CLAT 2027 syllabus is designed to test your reading comprehension, reasoning ability, logical thinking, and aptitude. The exam does not require prior knowledge of law, although staying updated with current legal and national issues can be helpful.

The CLAT 2027 exam consists of 120 MCQs to be completed in 2 hours. Each correct answer carries 1 mark, while 0.25 marks are deducted for every incorrect answer.

Important Points to Note:

  1. English, Legal Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, and Current Affairs are completely passage-based.
  2. Legal Reasoning does not require prior knowledge of law.
  3. Quantitative Techniques is based on Class 10 Mathematics and focuses mainly on data interpretation.
  4. Strong reading speed, comprehension, and reasoning skills are the most important factors for scoring well in CLAT 2027.

How to Apply For CLAT 2027

How to Apply For CLAT 2027 The entire application process will be conducted online mode. Four stages are involved while filling out the application form, i.e., online registration, filling out the form, uploading documents, and payment of the application fee.

Step 1: Visit the official website of CLAT, i.e.,

Step 2: On the Home page, click the link "register" and complete the registration process by entering the name and mobile number and setting a password.

Step 3: Enter the OTP received on the registered mobile number. This completes the registration process.

Step 4: Log in to the portal using the registered mobile number and password.

Step 5: Complete the application form by entering all the correct details.

Step 6: Upload necessary scanned images, documents, and signatures in the specified format.

Step 7: Pay the fee through the different online payment modes to complete the application process.

Documents to be uploaded with the Online Application.

  1. Front facing passport size recent photograph with plain background
  2. Signature of the candidate
  3. Category certificate if you are applying under SC/ST/OBC
  4. Relevant certificate issued by competent authority if candidate is applying under P.W.D
  5. Relevant certificate issued by competent authority if candidate is applying under BPL category

NOTE:

  1. The original certificates/documents issued by the competent authority as specified in the brochure are to be submitted to the allotted University in the form and manner as specified by the respective universities at the time of admission.
  2. The photograph and signature of the candidate need not be attested while submitting the Online Application Form.
  3. All documents/certificates should be uploaded in pdf format only.

Frequently Asked Questions!

In the CLAT UG 2027 exam, how many questions will be asked?

120 questions will be asked in the CLAT UG 2027 exam. Every question will carry one mark.

What is the exam pattern for CLAT 2027?

Will there be any negative markings in CLAT?

The CLAT 2027 exam is conducted in offline (pen-and-paper) mode and consists of 120 multiple-choice questions to be completed in 2 hours. Each correct answer carries 1 mark, and 0.25 marks are deducted for every incorrect answer. Will there be any negative markings in CLAT?

Is prior knowledge of law required for CLAT 2027?

Yes, in CLAT 2027, there will be a 0.25-mark penalty for each incorrect answer. No points will be deducted, however, for unanswered questions. Is prior knowledge of law required for CLAT 2027?

No. You do not need to study law before appearing for CLAT. The Legal Reasoning section tests your ability to understand legal principles and apply them to different situations rather than your knowledge of legal concepts.

Which colleges accept CLAT 2027 scores?

CLAT scores are accepted by 26 National Law Universities (NLUs) across India. In addition, many leading private law schools and universities also use CLAT 2027 scores for admission to their law programmes.

How many times can I attempt the CLAT exam?

There is no limit on the number of attempts for the CLAT exam. As long as you meet the CLAT 2027 eligibility criteria, you can appear for the exam as many times as you wish.
